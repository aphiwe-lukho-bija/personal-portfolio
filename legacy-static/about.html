About Me

I am a 23-year-old aspiring software developer currently studying software development and building practical experience through coding projects. I enjoy solving problems, learning new technologies, and creating applications that provide real-world value. One of my strengths is communication. I'm confident in public speaking and enjoy presenting technical concepts in a way that is clear and engaging.

Coding and everything that comes with it puts me in a flow state. When I am developing software, hours can pass without me noticing because I am fully immersed in the process of creating, learning, and solving problems.

Technology is shaping the future of the world, and being part of the software industry gives me a unique sense of purpose. Every new concept I learn and every project I build feels like a glimpse into what the future looks like before it arrives.

I thrive in team environments because I understand the value of collaboration and the impact that a strong team can have in the software industry. When creativity, technical skill, and diverse ways of thinking come together, they create opportunities for innovation.

Coding requires patience, discipline, resilience, dedication, and a genuine passion for continuous learning. Every challenge I encounter is an opportunity to grow, every mistake is a lesson, and every solution is a reminder of how rewarding persistence can be.

What excites me most is not only the technical knowledge I will gain, but also the qualities this journey will continue to build within me. I believe software development shapes character as much as it develops skill.

As I continue to grow in this field, I look forward to discovering new strengths, perspectives, and values that this path will add to who I am as both a developer and a person.

In addition to my studies, I have completed training as a GBVF Ambassador, which strengthened my leadership, advocacy, and communication skills.
