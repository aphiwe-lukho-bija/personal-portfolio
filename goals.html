My Goals

For me, goals are not simply milestones to be reached and left behind—they are a continuous process of growth, discipline, and refinement that I intend to pursue for as long as I am able. I see Software Development not only as a career path, but as a way of life.

I am currently a trainee at Life Choices Academy, where I am enrolled in the Full-Stack Software Development programme. One of my primary goals is to successfully complete this course within the expected timeframe.

However, I also understand that success in this field is not only defined by long-term goals, but also by the small daily habits that shape character and performance in the workplace.

In addition to technical growth, I place strong value on how I work with others. In a professional environment, I aim to be easy to work with, understanding, and open-minded.

Beyond this, I also have a strong interest in entrepreneurship. I am deeply drawn to the connection between Technology and Business, and I see it as a powerful combination that drives innovation.

At the same time, I have a competitive mindset that constantly pushes me forward. I am not in this industry to be average or to simply participate—I aim to compete with the best in the field.

In addition to this, I carry a strong sense of patriotism and responsibility toward my community, society, and country. I want my work in technology to be deeply connected to the people I come from.

My goal is to be one of the IT professionals who actively contributes to improving the lives of South Africans through meaningful technological solutions.

After completing my Full-Stack Software Development course at Life Choices Academy, I plan to further expand my skills by moving into Data Engineering.

To me, these goals are not separate—they form one continuous journey. Each stage builds on the previous one, and every skill I develop brings me closer to the bigger picture.
